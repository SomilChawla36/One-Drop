The Need

Why Is Blood Donation Important?

Hospitals rely on a steady, safe blood supply every single day. Here’s why your contribution matters.

Emergency Care

Accidents and trauma cases often require immediate transfusions to stabilize patients.

Major Surgeries

Complex procedures such as transplants and cardiac operations depend on ready blood supplies.

Chronic Illnesses

Patients with conditions like cancer or blood disorders often need regular transfusions.

Disaster Readiness

A well-stocked blood bank keeps communities prepared for large-scale emergencies.

How It’s Used

One Donation Can Make a Difference

A single donation is separated into components, so it can help more than one patient at a time.

1 Donation

A single unit collected from one donor.

Red Blood Cells

Carry oxygen; used for trauma and surgery.

Plasma

Supports clotting and burn treatment.

Platelets

Essential for cancer and surgical patients.

Multiple Patients

Each component can reach a different patient in need.

Getting Started

Who Can Donate?

General eligibility guidelines for blood donation.

✓ Meeting the minimum age requirement.
✓ Meeting the minimum weight requirement.
✓ Being in generally good health.
✓ Passing the donor screening process.
✓ Meeting the specific eligibility criteria of the blood donation center.

Note: exact age, weight, and health requirements can vary by country, region, and blood donation center. Always confirm the current criteria with your local center.

Not Sure If You're Eligible? Our medical staff will screen you before donation, so you don’t need to guess — just show up and ask.
